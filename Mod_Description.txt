[h1]Simple Vaccines[/h1]
[b]Now supports Build 42 and Build 41.[/b]

Are you tired of the uselessness of the First Aid skill in vanilla Project Zomboid? Simple Vaccines revamps the skill, letting you craft vaccines that give you a real chance of surviving a zombie bite.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Craftable zombie vaccines[/b] - four tiers, from boiled zombie cells to a near-perfect vaccine
[*] [b]Gives First Aid a purpose[/b] - better vaccines need higher First Aid, and every step trains it
[*] [b]Immunity builds up and fades[/b] - take boosters to stay protected
[*] [b]Custom animations[/b] (Build 42) - kneel at a corpse to extract DNA, inject a vaccine into your arm
[*] [b]New 3D models and icons[/b] (Build 42) - syringes and petri dishes, in your hands and on the ground
[*] [b]Sandbox options[/b] for vaccine strength, build-up and fade time, and DNA extraction
[*] [b]Multiplayer-ready[/b] (Build 42) - the server handles immunity and cures
[*] [b]10 languages[/b] - English, Spanish, Russian, Chinese, Brazilian Portuguese, German, French, Polish, Korean and Japanese
[/list]

[h2]How do I make a vaccine?[/h2]
[olist]
[*] Get a knife (any sharp knife works; on Build 41 a kitchen, hunting or flint knife) or, even better, a scalpel - a scalpel never wears out.
[*] Right-click a dead zombie and choose [b]Attempt to Extract DNA[/b]. Each corpse can only be sampled once (Build 42), and higher First Aid means better odds.
[*] Boil the zombie cells in a pot or saucepan of [b]very hot water[/b].
[*] Find syringes in hospitals, clinics, pharmacies and labs - or on zombie doctors, nurses and paramedics. Dirty syringes from bins and drug labs can be cleaned in hot water.
[*] Craft a vaccine from the boiled cells, an empty syringe, painkillers and water (higher tiers also need a poultice and antidepressants).
[*] Right-click the vaccine and choose [b]Inject[/b] (on Build 41, [b]Take[/b]).
[/olist]

[h2]How do vaccines work?[/h2]
After you take a vaccine, your immunity builds up over [b]7 days[/b], then slowly fades over the next [b]24 days[/b]. Take another dose as a booster to keep it up. You'll see a short message when a dose is taken and when a vaccine fights off an infection.

If you get bitten or scratched while immune, you get one chance to fight off the infection. That chance is your current immunity level.

[table]
[tr][th]Vaccine[/th][th]Protection at peak[/th][th]First Aid needed[/th][/tr]
[tr][td]Boiled Zombie Cells[/td][td]15%[/td][td]0[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]Crude Zombie Vaccine[/td][td]35%[/td][td]3[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]Simple Zombie Vaccine[/td][td]60%[/td][td]5[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]Perfect Zombie Vaccine[/td][td]95%[/td][td]8[/td][/tr]
[/table]
All numbers above are defaults and can be changed in the sandbox options.

[h2]Sandbox options[/h2]
[list]
[*] Effectiveness of each vaccine tier
[*] Days for immunity to build up / to fade (Build 42)
[*] DNA extraction chance, and extra chance per First Aid level (Build 42)
[*] One DNA extraction per corpse - on/off (Build 42)
[/list]

[h2]Optional: Moodle Framework[/h2]
If [b]Moodle Framework[/b] is installed, a moodle shows your immunity level. It isn't required. Note that Moodle Framework has had errors on recent Build 42 versions - if it breaks, Simple Vaccines turns the moodle off and keeps working.

[h2]Build 41 vs Build 42[/h2]
Build 41 keeps the original mod: vaccines are eaten, and DNA extraction removes the corpse. Everything new - animations, models, the Inject action, new sandbox options, multiplayer sync - is Build 42 only. Both versions have all 10 languages.

[h2]For mod makers[/h2]
Build 42 exposes a small API:
[list]
[*] [b]FAVUtils.getImmunity(player)[/b] - current chance (0-100) that the next infection is stopped
[*] [b]FAVUtils.isImmunityRising(player)[/b] - true while a dose is still building up
[*] [b]table.insert(FAVUtils.cureListeners, function(player) ... end)[/b] - called when a vaccine cures someone
[/list]
Works alongside mods that cure infection their own way (for example More Traits).
